First public release of ROM Roulette: a slot machine that picks a random game from your SD card.

- Spin the reel through every game your installed OnionOS emulators can see, stop it, and play what it lands on. The winner is drawn fairly from the pool when you stop.
- A game card before you play: title, system, region, box art from your `Imgs` folders, play counts, and the description, genre, year and developer when you've scraped a `gamelist.xml`.
- Pools: all games, favorites, never played, or one system.
- Played list and favorites.
- Games launch through OnionOS, so they appear in Recents, the GameSwitcher and Play Activity.
- Original 8-bit arcade sound effects (R2 turns them off).

**Install:** extract `ROMRoulette-v0.2.3-OnionOS.zip` and copy the `App` folder to the root of your SD card, merging it with the `App` folder already there. Then open **Apps → ROM Roulette**.
